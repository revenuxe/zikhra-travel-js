WebP assets have 320, 640 and 1200 pixel variants. Package cards use dedicated 4:5 portrait crops at quality 84 to avoid enlarging a narrow section of a landscape thumbnail. New photos are downloaded at 2400 pixels before cropping. Run `node scripts/optimize-travel-images.mjs` to regenerate.
